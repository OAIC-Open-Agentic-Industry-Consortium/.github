# Open Agentic Industry Consortium (OAIC)

**开放智能体产业联盟 · Open · Open Source · Collaborative**

[Website](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=en) · [Whitepapers](https://www.oaic.ai/resources/whitepapers/?lang=en) · [Build with us](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=en#join) · [简体中文](#中文)

## About OAIC

OAIC is an open industry collaboration platform connecting enterprises, universities, open-source communities, standards bodies, developers, and startups. We work to build neutral infrastructure and a global collaboration ecosystem for the agentic AI era, rooted in China and open to the world.

Starting with real industry needs, we connect theory, reference architectures, open-source implementations, pre-standardization, Testbeds, and industry cases. Our agenda spans enterprise transformation, community and talent incubation, responsible AI, and international collaboration.

## Agentic AI Factory

Our capability platform brings together five public capabilities, with **Testbed validation across all five**:

| Capability | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **AOS — Agentic Orchestration System** | Multi-agent runtime, lifecycle, coordination, secure execution, protocol interoperability, resources, and state. |
| **ASE — Agentic Software Engineering** | Engineering and quality across requirements, development, testing, deployment, and operations, including Harness and Loop Engineering. |
| **ASG — Agentic Semantic Ground** | A persistent, governed operating map connecting data, knowledge, context, decisions, actions, outcomes, and evidence. |
| **Open Token** | Open metering and governance protocols for discoverable capabilities, enforceable budgets, verifiable usage, and traceable receipts. |
| **OAI Marketplace** | Publishing, discovering, combining, evaluating, and reusing agentic capabilities, tools, services, templates, and scenario outcomes. |

These capabilities form an open collaboration framework. Open Token is a protocol family for metering and governance, not a currency or financial asset.

Explore the [capability platform](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=en#platform) and the [Open Source Agentic AI Landscape](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=en#architecture), covering projects, interfaces, and standards across the agentic stack.

## Open programs

Our launch agenda connects requirements, architecture, implementation, testing, standards, and industry cases through six programs:

- **HAgency Open Collaboration & Testbed** — Connect real scenarios, open projects, and end-to-end validation, with feedback driving revisions and reusable cases.
- **Open AgentOS** — Build an open AOS reference implementation, foundational system capabilities, and assistant Testbeds.
- **ASE Framework & Methodology** — Develop engineering theory, maturity models, and Harness and Loop engineering methods.
- **Specifications, Testing & Validation** — Derive interfaces, semantics, test vectors, conformance requirements, and open validation methods from Testbeds.
- **Agent Innovation Lab (AIL)** — Connect universities, communities, researchers, and developers to grow talent and projects.
- **Thought Leadership & Industry Positions** — Develop industry insight, issue papers, the AI for Humanity initiative, and global technical dialogue.

See the [open programs](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=en#programs) and [2026–2029 roadmap](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=en#roadmap).

## 中文

### 关于 OAIC

**开放智能体产业联盟（Open Agentic Industry Consortium，OAIC）** 是面向 Agentic AI 的开放产业协作平台，连接企业、高校、开源社区、标准组织、开发者与创业团队。

我们致力于构建智能体 AI 时代的中立基础设施与全球协作生态。以真实产业需求为牵引，把理论、参考架构、开源实现、标准预研、Testbed 与行业案例连接起来，推动企业转型、社区与人才孵化、负责任 AI 和国际合作。

**立足中国，面向世界。共建开放、中立、可验证的智能体产业协作生态。**

### AI 智能体工场

五类公共能力协同工作，**Testbed 验证贯穿其中**：

| 能力 | 共建方向 |
| --- | --- |
| **AOS · 智能体编排系统** | 多智能体运行与编排、生命周期、协同调度、安全执行、协议互通、资源治理和状态管理。 |
| **ASE · 智能体软件工程** | 覆盖需求、开发、测试、部署与运维的工程和质量体系，包括 Harness 与 Loop 工程方法。 |
| **ASG · 智能体语义底座** | 连接数据、知识、上下文、决策、行动、结果与证据，构建持久、可治理的动态运营地图。 |
| **Open Token · 开放计量与治理** | 支持能力发现、预算约束、用量核验与回执追溯的开放协议族。 |
| **OAI Marketplace · 成果协作** | 支持智能体能力、工具、服务、模板与场景成果的发布、发现、组合、评价和复用。 |

这些能力共同构成开放协作框架。Open Token 是计量与治理协议，不是货币或金融资产。

了解[能力平台](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=zh#platform)，查看覆盖智能体技术栈各层项目、接口与标准的[开源智能体 AI 全景图](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=zh#architecture)。

### 六项启动期开放计划

- **HAgency 开放项目协作与 Testbed**：连接真实场景、开放项目与端到端验证，推动问题回流、版本修订与行业案例沉淀。
- **Open AgentOS**：共建开放 AOS 参考实现、系统能力与终端助手验证环境。
- **ASE 体系与方法论**：沉淀智能体软件工程理论、成熟度模型、Harness 与 Loop 工程方法。
- **规范、测试与验证**：从 Testbed 提炼接口、语义、测试向量、符合性要求与公开验证方法。
- **智能体创新实验室（AIL）**：连接高校、开源社区、研究者与开发者，培育人才和创新项目。
- **思想领导力与产业主张**：形成产业洞察、议题白皮书、AI for Humanity 倡议与国际技术对话。

了解[开放项目](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=zh#programs)及 [2026—2029 路线图](https://www.oaic.ai/?lang=zh#roadmap)。
